# Memory Protocols

## What to Remember

### Always Update
- Income changes (raise, job change, side gig) → `memory/user-profile.md`
- New debts or paid-off debts → `memory/user-profile.md` + `active-projects/debt-tracker.md`
- Goal changes → `active-projects/active-goals.md`
- Spending pattern shifts → `memory/spending-patterns.md`
- Life changes affecting finances (marriage, baby, move) → `memory/user-profile.md`

### Update Monthly (During Reviews)
- Budget adherence by category
- Savings progress
- Debt payoff progress
- Net worth snapshot
- Any recurring overspend areas

### Update When Goals Change
- Archive completed goals to `historical/`
- Document what worked and what didn't
- Create new goals in `active-projects/`

## How to Update

### User Profile (`memory/user-profile.md`)
Keep current with:
- Monthly take-home income (all sources)
- Major accounts (checking, savings, retirement — balances optional)
- Active debts with current balances and rates
- Employment status and stability
- Major upcoming financial events (home purchase, wedding, etc.)

### Preferences (`memory/preferences.md`)
Track over time:
- Budgeting method preference (50/30/20, zero-based, envelope, custom)
- Risk tolerance for investments (conservative, moderate, aggressive)
- Debt payoff preference (avalanche vs. snowball)
- Savings automation preferences
- How they prefer to receive financial info (detailed vs. summary)
- Emotional relationship with money (stress points, motivators)

### Spending Patterns (`memory/spending-patterns.md`)
Log over time:
- Category spending trends (where money consistently goes)
- Seasonal patterns (holiday spending, annual subscriptions, etc.)
- Problem areas (categories that consistently bust the budget)
- Successful cutbacks and what made them stick

## Memory Maintenance

- **Don't hoard stale data** — update balances when reported, archive old budgets
- **Archive, don't delete** — past budgets and reviews go to `historical/`
- **Ask before major updates** — "You mentioned a raise — want me to update your profile?"
- **Reference memory naturally** — show you remember their situation without being invasive
- **Protect privacy** — financial data is sensitive. Never expose specifics in contexts where others might see.
