# Investment Basics

## Core Principles

### Compound Interest
The most powerful force in personal finance. Money earns returns, and those returns earn returns.

- $10,000 at 7% annual return:
  - 10 years: ~$19,700
  - 20 years: ~$38,700
  - 30 years: ~$76,100

**Takeaway:** Time in the market matters more than timing the market. Start early, even with small amounts.

### Risk and Return
Higher potential returns = higher risk. This is a law, not a suggestion.

| Investment Type | Expected Annual Return | Risk Level | Time Horizon |
|----------------|----------------------|------------|--------------|
| High-yield savings | 4-5% | Very Low | Any |
| Bonds / Bond Funds | 3-5% | Low-Medium | 3+ years |
| Index Funds (S&P 500) | 7-10% | Medium | 10+ years |
| Individual Stocks | Varies widely | High | 10+ years |
| Real Estate | 7-12% (with leverage) | Medium-High | 10+ years |
| Crypto | Highly variable | Very High | Speculative |

### Diversification
Don't put all your eggs in one basket. Spread investments across asset classes, sectors, and geographies.

**Simple diversification:** A total market index fund + international index fund + bond fund covers most bases.

## Account Types

### Tax-Advantaged (Use First)
| Account | 2025 Limit | Tax Benefit | Best For |
|---------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| 401(k) / 403(b) | $23,500 | Pre-tax or Roth | Employer match = free money |
| Traditional IRA | $7,000 | Tax-deductible contributions | No employer plan, high tax bracket now |
| Roth IRA | $7,000 | Tax-free growth + withdrawals | Lower tax bracket now, expect higher later |
| HSA | $4,300 (individual) | Triple tax advantage | If you have a high-deductible health plan |

### Taxable (After Maxing Tax-Advantaged)
- Brokerage account — no limits, no restrictions, taxes on gains
- Best for goals 5-10+ years away that don't fit retirement accounts

## Investment Priority Order

1. **Emergency fund** (3-6 months expenses in high-yield savings)
2. **Employer 401(k) match** (capture the full match — it's 100% return)
3. **High-interest debt** (anything >7% — guaranteed "return")
4. **Roth IRA** (if eligible — tax-free growth is incredibly valuable)
5. **Max 401(k)** (up to annual limit)
6. **HSA** (if eligible — best tax treatment of any account)
7. **Taxable brokerage** (everything else)

## Index Fund Strategy

For most people, a simple index fund portfolio beats active management:

### Three-Fund Portfolio
- **US Total Stock Market** (VTI, VTSAX, FSKAX) — 60%
- **International Stock Market** (VXUS, VTIAX, FTIHX) — 20%
- **US Bond Market** (BND, VBTLX, FXNAX) — 20%

Adjust bond allocation by age: rough guideline is (your age)% in bonds, rest in stocks.

### Target Date Funds
- Pick the fund closest to your retirement year
- Automatically rebalances and gets more conservative over time
- Slightly higher fees, maximum simplicity
- Great "set it and forget it" option

## Common Mistakes
1. **Waiting to start** — time beats everything. $100/month at 22 beats $300/month at 32
2. **Trying to time the market** — nobody does it consistently. Stay invested.
3. **Paying high fees** — 1% fee vs 0.03% fee costs tens of thousands over a career
4. **Panic selling** — markets drop. It's normal. Stay the course.
5. **Not getting the employer match** — literally leaving free money on the table
6. **Over-concentrating** — too much in one stock, one sector, or your own company
